# Preserve approved visual intent

Inputs: actual reference/version, approvals, delegated decisions.

1. Inspect the reference. Preserve its location/version, layout, action hierarchy,
   visibility, responsiveness and essential states in an authorized permanent
   project record. Apply latest explicit changes; never mix versions. Missing
   reference: request it; pause dependent work only.
2. Separate requirements, structure, appearance and technical approvals.
   Wireframes approve shown structure, not unseen styling. Before implementing
   unapproved appearance, present an accessible concrete design covering key
   states and responsiveness; obtain approval unless delegated. Opening queued
   proves neither viewing nor approval.
3. Preserve authorizations. Obtain approval for material deviations before
   implementation. Direct instructions authorize named changes; conformance fixes
   and delegated details need no repeated approval.
4. Compare actual renders with the reference at relevant sizes/states. Verify
   behavior, structure and appearance separately; functional tests or screenshots
   alone do not prove conformance.
5. Report evidence, approved deviations and unverified aspects. Unapproved
   material mismatches prevent full-completion claims.

Before preserving records follow
`harness/common/skills/comm-preserve-harness-knowledge/SKILL.md`; evidence also
follows `harness/common/skills/comm-protect-harness-evidence/SKILL.md`.

Done: demonstrated conformance or explicit outstanding mismatches/limits.
Preserve the selected workflow; keep product-specific business rules in project.
