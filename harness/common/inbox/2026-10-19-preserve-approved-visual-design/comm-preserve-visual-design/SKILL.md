# Preserve approved visual intent

Inputs: current reference/version, requirements, approvals and delegated decisions.

1. Inspect the actual reference; record layout, hierarchy, visibility, responsive
   behavior and essential states. Apply explicit user changes. Missing reference:
   seek only missing information; do not substitute an invented design.
2. Distinguish requirements, structure, appearance and technical approvals.
   Before implementing unapproved appearance, present a concrete design and obtain
   approval unless already delegated. A wireframe approves its structure, not
   unseen styling. Tool opening queued is not viewing or approval.
3. Preserve existing authorizations. Approve material deviations before dependent
   implementation; direct user instructions authorize their specified changes.
   Routine conformance fixes and delegated details need no repeat approval.
4. Compare actual rendered screens with the approved reference at relevant sizes
   and states. Check behavior separately from structure and appearance. Tests of
   the implementation alone do not establish conformance.
5. Report conformance evidence, approved deviations and unverified aspects.
   Material unapproved mismatches prevent a full-completion claim.

Before preserving artifacts follow
`harness/common/skills/comm-preserve-harness-knowledge/SKILL.md`; evidence also
follows `harness/common/skills/comm-protect-harness-evidence/SKILL.md`.

Done: demonstrated conformance within approved scope, or explicit outstanding
mismatches/verification limits. Preserve the selected development workflow.
